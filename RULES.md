# Last of the Kilmore Oaks — Rules

You are the forest. The last Kilmore Oaks are taking back the city that paved over them. Overrun all six districts
before the Steward, the city's AI, contains you.

## Skill shots

Plunge the ball and:

- **Drop targets:** hit the lit drop target.
- **Left pop:** hit the left pop bumper.
- **Secret:** find it.

## Overgrow the city

Climb the city from the riverfront to the top: three tiers, with a gate between each. You play one place on each
step. Places marked * are multiballs.

| Step | Choose |
|---|---|
| 1. Tier 1 | Meridian Basins **or** Cargo Quay |
| 2. Gate 1 | Nimbus Promenade |
| 3. Tier 2 | Hydro Core **or** Helix Vaults* **or** Skyrail Station |
| 4. Tier 3 | Apex Terraces **or** Halcyon Vents* |
| 5. Gate 2 | Caldera Generator* |
| 6. Finale | Solarium Atrium |

**Light the scoop.** Main shots with a white center are lit, and go out when you hit them. Hit 2 to light the left
scoop, then 3 the next time, then 4, and so on. A Surveillance set counts as one.

**Choose a place.** Shoot the lit left scoop to open the menu. The flippers move, and both flippers (or Start)
choose.

- Choosing a place closes the others on that step for the rest of the game.
- A place ends when you complete it or drain. An unfinished place stays in the menu, and you can resume it with its
  progress.
- **Completing a district** earns a perk for the rest of the game. The multiballs don't have one.

## Districts

Each district plays on the six main shots: left orbit, dome ramp, arc ramp, center orbit, lift ramp and right orbit.
The Steward's announcement at the start tells you what to shoot.

**Meridian Basins — green.** Two shots start flashing. Hit a flashing shot to bloom it, and the growth spreads to the
shots on either side. Bloom all 6. Go 15 seconds without a bloom and the last one withers.

**Cargo Quay — orange.** The lift ramp is up: it's the crane. Two containers sit on shots. Hit a container's shot to
push it one shot toward the crane, and containers that meet stack and move together. Once both are in, shoot the
flashing crane.

**Hydro Core — cyan.** Make the combo in order: lift ramp, arc ramp, left orbit, center orbit, dome ramp. Each shot
after the first has 25 seconds. Run out and the combo starts over, with 5 more seconds each try.

**Nimbus Promenade — magenta.** Every shot is an open walkway. Hit a magenta walkway to weaken it (purple), then hit
it again to close it (dark). The Steward blocks walkways for a few seconds, flashing them red first, and a red walkway
can't be hit. Close 3 walkways.

**Skyrail Station — blue.** A station flashes, then the train races to it. Hit the station before the train gets there
for double points, or after for three quarters. Destroy 4 stations to finish, and beat the train to all 4 for a
perfect bonus.

**Apex Terraces — gold.** One shot is lit. Each hit fills its ring. A full ring bursts the terrace, and the lit shot
jumps to the far side. Take too long and the ring drains. Terraces need 3, then 4, then 5 hits. Burst 3.

**Solarium Atrium — purple.** The vine's head moves from shot to shot, and the dim shot is where it goes next. Each
hit speeds it up. Hit it 6 times.

### Perks

| Complete | Perk |
|---|---|
| Meridian Basins | Roots spread twice as fast |
| Hydro Core | +5 seconds on all mode timers |
| Nimbus Promenade | Pops charge spores twice as fast |
| Skyrail Station | Every drone downed lights a lock |
| Apex Terraces | Center spinner values ×2 |
| Solarium Atrium | Captive ball charges Heartwood twice as fast |

## Multiball places

These places play as a multiball. One ends when you're back to one ball, except Helix Vaults, which carries on.

- **Helix Vaults** (2-ball): orbits drain the vat. After enough, a ramp lights for a rupture. Rupture it 3 times.
- **Halcyon Vents** (2-ball): spinners and pops build the value. The ramps vent it. Vent 3 times.
- **Caldera Generator** (2-ball): each shot you hit goes dark. When they all are, the left scoop is the super jackpot.

## Side quests

These run all the time, alongside the districts.

- **Charge — green.** Spinner spins and pops fill the spore bar. A full bar lights the left inlane target. Hit it to
  fire a **spore burst**: for 8 seconds every district shot counts double. With no district running, it scores big
  points.
- **Surveillance — red.** The pop targets are cameras. They come online after 40 playfield switch hits, and stay on
  for the rest of your game. Blind all three for a set. Each set needs one more hit per camera, counts toward
  lighting the scoop, and every third set lights an outlane save. Once they're online, blind a camera at least once
  a minute, or the Steward sends the Sentinels.
- **Sentinels — red.** The drones take over: half the playfield lights go red, and whatever district is running
  waits. The lift ramp goes up to feed the upper flipper. Clear the drop targets to down a drone, and down them all
  to get back to it. The drones sent go 1, 1, 2, 2, 3, and so on. Draining ends them, but the next time still counts
  up. Every 2 drones lights a lock. At 5
  drones: points. 10: extra ball lit. 15: big points. *(These rewards are left over from the old Sentinels and will
  be revisited.)*
- **Heartwood — lime.** Hit the captive ball to charge the heart. A full charge starts **Heartwood multiball**. It
  needs more each time.
- **Spread roots — brown.** Orbits grow roots around that shot's ring. A full ring arms **Root Lash**: press the
  action button during a mode for 2 shots in it. The roots then rest for 50 switch hits. The rings start over each
  ball, and spreading all three orbits in one ball lights an extra ball. Spreading all three over the game lights
  **Groundswell**.

## Combos

Make a route of shots in a row, each within about 4 seconds of the last. Each route pays once per game.

| Route | Shots |
|---|---|
| Interchange | Dome ramp → lift ramp |
| Return trip | Lift ramp → dome ramp |
| Express line | Lift ramp → arc ramp |
| Roundabout | Right orbit → right orbit |
| Ring road | Right orbit → lift ramp |
| Underpass | Arc ramp → lift ramp |
| Cloverleaf | Dome ramp → lift ramp → dome ramp |
| Grand tour | Dome ramp → right orbit → lift ramp |

Make all 8 for **Gridlock**. The lift ramp only counts with the ramp down.

## ArcStreak

Hit the ball straight off the lower scoop's eject up the arc ramp for a **reflex shot**: 2M, then 3M, then 5M for each
one after. The ramp feeds back to the scoop, so keep looping it. Miss one and the streak starts over.

## Multiballs

- **Groundswell.** 2-ball. Spread the roots on all three orbits, over any number of balls, to light it at the left
  scoop: no lit shots needed, and it doesn't close anything on the map. The lift ramp goes up and feeds the upper
  flipper. The drops are the city's foundations: each target is a jackpot, and each bank clear breaks one. Break 3.
  Each time after, lighting it takes one more spread on every orbit.
- **Heartwood.** 2-ball, from a full Heartwood charge. Main shots are jackpots. Make the arc ramp to the subway twice
  to add a ball.

## Saves

- **Ball save** at the start of every ball, multiball and district.
- **Outlane saves.** Sling hits grow the roots up that side's posts. When they're full, that outlane is lit, and the
  next drain through it is saved. A lit save keeps until you use it, and each use makes that side harder to relight.
- **Extra balls** are lit by completing your first multiball place, 10 drones, and spreading the roots on all three orbits in one ball. Collect a lit extra ball with a shot to the front of the
  left scoop.
