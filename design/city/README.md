# The city

The main progression: the forest climbs Cascadia Prime one place at a time. One file per place. Each says what the place is, how its mode plays, and how the lights, sound and display sell it. The story behind the city is in [story.md](../story.md).

Work through a place in this order:

1. **Purpose.** What the place is, and the one thing it's known for.
2. **Gameplay.** The rules, built around that one thing.
3. **Lighting.** What the playfield shows while the mode runs.
4. **Sound and display.** A few notes are enough until the first three are settled.

## The map

Every mode is a place on the city map. The city is laid out in three tiers by height, with a gate between each, and the climb ends in a final wizard mode.

```
                 FINALE (wizard mode)
      Solarium Atrium  →  Spire Core
         phase 1: the reveal   phase 2: the Steward's core
                   ▲
          ═══ Caldera Generator ═══   gate 2: the power falls
                   ▲
   TIER 3  The Mountain
      Apex Terraces · Halcyon Vents
                   ▲
   TIER 2  The Mid-City
      Hydro Core · Helix Vaults · Skyrail Station
                   ▲
          ═══ Nimbus Promenade ═══    gate 1: the barriers fall
                   ▲
   TIER 1  The Riverfront
      Meridian Basins · Cargo Quay
```

- **Tier 1, the Riverfront.** The forest takes root quietly in the low, wet places: the farms and the freight docks. The Steward treats it as contamination, a maintenance problem.
- **Gate 1, Nimbus Promenade.** The Steward realizes it's more than maintenance and shuts the city. The forest forces its way through.
- **Tier 2, the Mid-City.** The forest goes after the city's water, its vats and its transit. The mystery starts to come out at the Helix Vaults.
- **Tier 3, the Mountain.** The forest climbs to the wealthy, and to the vents that make the city's air.
- **Gate 2, Caldera Generator.** The power goes out sector by sector. The Steward starts to come apart, and the only light left is the roots.
- **The finale.** One wizard mode in two phases. In the dark, the Solarium reveals the truth, and in the Spire Core the forest reaches the Steward itself.

Halcyon Vents moved up to tier 3: air plants on the mountain, in the thin clean air, and it takes over the wind scrubbers that Apex Terraces' story had.

### Game length

There are more places than before, so a full run is longer. Ways to keep it in check:

- Make the map wider rather than deeper: more choice within a tier, not more tiers.
- Make extra balls easier to earn.

### The playfield

The playfield is a whitewood with temporary art, so the next version can have an insert per place.

## The mystery

Every place in tiers 2 and 3, and the Caldera Generator, drops a clue toward what the finale reveals. Some are small and some are big, and a player has to play them all to get every piece. See [mystery.md](../mystery.md).

### In the game

The game follows this map. A player plays one place on each step, a tier or a gate, and choosing one closes the rest
of that step. Multiball places qualify like districts. A game covers 6 of the 10 places.
