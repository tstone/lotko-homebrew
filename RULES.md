# Last of the Kilmore Oaks — Rules

You are the forest. The last Kilmore Oaks are taking back the city that paved over them. Overrun all six districts
before the Steward, the city's AI, contains you.

## Skill shots

Plunge the ball and:

- **Drop targets:** hit the lit drop target.
- **Left pop:** hit the left pop bumper.
- **Secret:** find it.

## Overgrow the city

The city map shows the six districts. You play at most one district on each step, and the route you take decides what
comes next:

| Step | Choose |
|---|---|
| 1 | Meridian Basins **or** Hydro Core |
| 2 | Nimbus Promenade |
| 3 | Mini-wizard: Floodgates (after Meridian) or Blackout (after Hydro) |
| 4 | Skyrail Station **or** Apex Terraces |
| 5 | Solarium Atrium |
| 6 | Mini-wizard: Derailment (after Skyrail) or Canopy (after Apex) |

**Light the scoop.** Main shots with a white center are lit, and go out when you hit them. Hit 2 to light the left
scoop, then 3 the next time, then 4, and so on. A Surveillance set counts as one.

**Choose a district.** Shoot the lit left scoop to open the menu. The flippers move, and both flippers (or Start)
choose. A mini-wizard is lit as soon as you reach its step.

- Choosing a district closes the other one on that step for the rest of the game.
- A district ends when you complete it or drain. An unfinished district stays in the menu, and you can resume it with
  its progress.
- **Completing a district** earns a perk for the rest of the game.

## Districts

Each district plays on the six main shots: left orbit, dome ramp, arc ramp, center orbit, lift ramp and right orbit.
The Steward's announcement at the start tells you what to shoot.

**Meridian Basins — orange.** Three neighboring shots are lit, and the window moves right every couple of seconds.
Hit lit shots 6 times.

**Hydro Core — cyan.** Make the combo in order: lift ramp, arc ramp, left orbit, center orbit, dome ramp. Each shot
after the first has 25 seconds. Run out and the combo starts over, with 5 more seconds each try.

**Nimbus Promenade — magenta.** Every shot is an open walkway. The Steward seals one at a time and it goes dark (it
flickers first). Hit an open walkway to choke it green for good. Hit a sealed one to reopen it. Choke 4 walkways.

**Skyrail Station — blue.** Lift ramp, drops, lift ramp, drops, lift ramp. The third ramp shot adds a ball. Then hit
5 drop targets to finish.

**Apex Terraces — gold.** One shot is lit. Each hit fills its ring. A full ring bursts the terrace, and the lit shot
jumps to the far side. Take too long and the ring drains. Terraces need 3, then 4, then 5 hits. Burst 3.

**Solarium Atrium — purple.** The vine's head moves from shot to shot, and the dim shot is where it goes next. Each
hit speeds it up. Hit it 6 times.

### Perks

| Complete | Perk |
|---|---|
| Meridian Basins | Root tunnels dig twice as fast |
| Hydro Core | +5 seconds on all mode timers |
| Nimbus Promenade | Pops charge spores twice as fast |
| Skyrail Station | Every drone downed lights a lock |
| Apex Terraces | Center spinner values ×2 |
| Solarium Atrium | Captive ball charges Heartwood twice as fast |

## Mini-wizards

Multiball modes at the end of each tier. Tier 1 is 2-ball, tier 2 is 3-ball and worth more. A mini-wizard ends when
you're back to one ball.

- **Floodgates:** orbits raise the water. At high water, the ramps are super jackpots.
- **Blackout:** each shot you hit goes dark. When they all are, the left scoop is the super jackpot.
- **Derailment:** feed the upper flipper and shoot the drops for jackpots.
- **Canopy:** spinners and pops build the value. The ramps collect it.

## Side quests

These run all the time, alongside the districts.

- **Charge.** Spinner spins and pops fill the spore bar. A full bar lights the left inlane target. Hit it to fire a
  **spore burst**: for 8 seconds every district shot counts double. With no district running, it scores big points.
- **Surveillance.** The red pop targets are cameras. Blind all three for a set. Each set needs one more hit per camera,
  counts toward lighting the scoop, and every third set lights an outlane save.
- **Sentinels.** Clear the drop targets to down a drone. Every 2 drones lights a lock. At 5 drones: points. 10: extra
  ball lit. 15: big points.
- **Heartwood.** Hit the captive ball to charge the heart. A full charge starts **Heartwood multiball**. It needs more
  each time.
- **Root tunnels.** Orbits dig a tunnel at that shot's ring. A full ring scores and counts as a hit in the current
  district.

## Combos

Make a route of shots in a row, each within about 4 seconds of the last. Each route pays once per game.

| Route | Shots |
|---|---|
| Interchange | Dome ramp → lift ramp |
| Return trip | Lift ramp → dome ramp |
| Express line | Lift ramp → arc ramp |
| Roundabout | Right orbit → right orbit |
| Ring road | Right orbit → lift ramp |
| Underpass | Arc ramp → lift ramp |
| Cloverleaf | Dome ramp → lift ramp → dome ramp |
| Grand tour | Dome ramp → right orbit → lift ramp |

Make all 8 for **Gridlock**. The lift ramp only counts with the ramp down.

## Multiballs

- **Groundswell.** Lock lit raises the lift ramp. Shoot it to lock. Lock 3 for 3-ball multiball: jackpots are on the
  main shots, and the super jackpot is the lift ramp, then the drops from the upper flipper. Your first super jackpot
  lights an extra ball.
- **Heartwood.** 2-ball, from a full Heartwood charge. Main shots are jackpots.

## Saves

- **Ball save** at the start of every ball, multiball and district.
- **Outlane saves.** Sling hits grow the roots up that side's posts. When they're full, that outlane is lit, and the
  next drain through it is saved. A lit save keeps until you use it, and each use makes that side harder to relight.
- **Extra balls** are lit by your first district completion, 10 drones and your first Groundswell super jackpot.
  Collect a lit extra ball with a shot to the front of the left scoop.
