# Caldera Generator

**Status:** new place, built from the Blackout mini-wizard.

## The place

The city's geothermal power plant. Cascadia Prime sits on the volcanic arc of the Pacific Northwest, and the city draws its power from the heat of the dormant volcano it's built against. The plant is set into the caldera, the volcano's crater. The same mountain holds Apex Terraces, so the city's richest homes sit on the slopes of the volcano that powers everything below them.

The forest's roots reach deeper than the city's boreholes, and they choke the heat at its source.

## Signature

The lights going out. The grid goes black sector by sector, and as it does the Steward's calm comes apart.

## Gameplay

**Today (Blackout, 2-ball multiball):** each shot you hit goes dark. When they all are, the left scoop is the super jackpot.

**Direction:** keep it. It's the most memorable mini-wizard, and its lighting is the reason.

## Lighting

**Today:** the shot and its section of the playfield go dark in hard steps. The super jackpot is a second of total dark, then a wave of light from the spinner.

**Direction:** the dark is the star. Push it further:

- The sectors stay dark for the rest of the mode, so the playfield gets darker as the player wins.
- Only the roots glow in the dark.

## Sound

- A heavy clunk as each sector goes dark.
- A descending power-down whine.
- The Steward breaking up more with each sector. Only "System 67 invalid" is recorded so far.

## Display

- A city grid with sectors going black.
- The Steward's animation glitching as the power fails.
