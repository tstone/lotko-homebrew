# Reflex shots

**Status:** one built (ArcStreak). Room for more.

A reflex shot is hit straight off an eject, before the player has time to trap the ball.

## ArcStreak (today)

Hit the ball straight off the lower scoop's eject and up the arc ramp, within about 2 seconds of the eject. The arc ramp feeds the subway back to the scoop, so a good player can loop it.

| Reflex shot | Award |
|---|---|
| First | 2,000,000 |
| Second | 3,000,000 |
| Each after | 5,000,000 |

- The streak breaks when an eject goes by without a reflex shot, when the ball comes into the scoop any way but the subway, or when the ball ends.
- It runs alongside modes and combos.
