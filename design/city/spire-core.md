## Gameplay

**For now:** Apex Terraces' old rules, parked here until the place is designed.

1. One shot is lit. Each hit fills its ring.
2. A full ring breaks through, and the lit shot jumps to the far side.
3. Take too long and the ring drains.
4. The first needs 3 hits, then 4, then 5.
5. Break through 3 to complete it.

One reading: breaking through the government's security, with the Steward locking it again if the player is too slow.
