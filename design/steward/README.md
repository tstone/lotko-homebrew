# The Steward

The negative progression: the city fighting back. The Steward watches the playfield through its cameras, and when the forest goes unchecked it sends the Sentinels.

1. [Surveillance](surveillance.md): the cameras come online, and the player has to keep blinding them.
2. [Sentinels](sentinels.md): if the player doesn't, the drones take over the playfield.

The Steward is red. Anything it does to the playfield is lit red, and red means the player can't make that shot count right now.
