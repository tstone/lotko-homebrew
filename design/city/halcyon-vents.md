# Halcyon Vents

**Status:** built as the old Canopy (renamed), the tier 3 multiball. The direction below is sketched.

## The place

The plant that makes the city's air: oxygen generators and great air scrubbers, with turbines drawing air in and pushing it out clean across the city.

The forest made the air once. When the founders cleared it, they had to build machines to breathe for them. The forest taking the plant back is the canopy returning to do the job it always did.

"Halcyon" means calm and peaceful, which is how the Steward sells the place. The forest makes it wild.

## Signature

The turbines, and the city breathing.

## Why Canopy fits

Canopy's spinners become the plant's turbines, building up air, and the ramps vent it. Its story (the forest's canopy spreading over the city) becomes this place's ending: the trees take back the job of making the air.

## Gameplay

A 3-ball multiball, carried over from Canopy.

1. The vertical spinner, the center spinner and the pops are the turbines. Each spin and pop builds the value.
2. The ramps are lit. A ramp vents the air and collects the value, which then starts building again from the base.
3. Vent 3 times to complete it.
4. It ends when one ball is left.

## Lighting

**Today (Canopy):** the ramps glow in the mode color, and a collect flashes the ramp's ring green.

**Direction:**

- **The GI breathes.** The whole playfield slowly brightens and dims, like breathing. This is a place that makes special use of the GI.
- **Turbines:** the spinners and pops light up as they spin, and the faster the value builds, the faster the breathing.
- **Vent:** a rush of light from the ramp, sweeping across the playfield.
- **Completion:** the breathing turns from the city's colors to the forest's green.
- **Idea from Canopy:** a forest field that climbs the playfield and carries on up into the backbox and the speaker rings.

## Sound

The plant has an obvious background sound: the deep hum of turbines and the rush of moving air.

- Turbines spinning up as the value builds.
- A vent: a big exhale of air.
- Recorded: `oak/higher`, written for Canopy.

## Display

- **Start:** the turbines and stacks of the plant.
- **Completion:** the turbines slowing, and leaves where the machines were.
