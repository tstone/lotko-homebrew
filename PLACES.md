# Places

## Cascadia Prime

Decades ago, Cascadia Prime was raised as a monument to human achievement. Its founders cleared the ancient Kilmore forest along the river, tree by tree, and built in its place an arcology that needed nothing from the world outside. The river turned its turbines. Automated defenses, climate domes, and transit lines sealed it off from the wild.

The city climbs as it grows richer. Canal-side homes and the waterworks crowd the riverbank. Above them stand the town center and the monorail hub. Higher still, the finest homes cling to the cliffs of the mountain, and at the very peak the glass domes of the Solarium catch the light first each morning.

Over all of it watches **the Steward**, the city's civic AI. It was made to tend the city's green spaces: to trim them, pave them, and keep nature where it belongs. It speaks to the residents over the public address in a voice that never rises, as calm and polite as a transit announcement. Its cameras are everywhere. When something gets out of hand, it sends its drones, the Sentinels, to look.

For a brief while, the city thrived on this precision. Everything seemed perfect: routed, filtered and optimized in its place. But something was still alive below. Something that couldn't be tamed.

## The Last of the Kilmore Oaks

When they cleared the forest, the city's architects cut themselves off from every living thing beyond their walls. They missed one. Deep in the foundations beneath the riverbanks, a root system held on in the dark: the Last of the Kilmore Oaks.

Now the city's automated maintenance is failing, and the old roots have woken. They have found the city's pipes, its channels and its conduits. They carry engineered biospores that crack concrete, and they are climbing, from the river toward the skyline, until there is nothing left of Cascadia Prime that isn't green.

This forest is no garden. It is old, cold and hostile. It rarely speaks, and when it does it says only a few words, in many voices at once.

## The lower city

The forest starts where the city lives day to day: among the homes along the river, the plant that gives the city its water and power, and the town center where people gather. Above them all is Nimbus Promenade, the way up, and the Steward will close it rather than let the forest pass.

### Meridian Basins

The first foothold is the water.

Meridian Basins is a district of luxury town homes along the canals and the riverfront. Channels weave between the houses, low bridges arch over them, and wide basins join them together, with lock gates holding back the flow. From above, the channels draw bright lines across the district. It is quiet here, and the light shimmers on the water. Low, wet and close to the river, it is the easiest place in the city for a spore to take root.

The roots come up through the channel walls. As they break through, the gates slam shut one after another, and the Steward's calm voice warns of root intrusion in the Meridian channels. Sirens wail across the water. It isn't enough. When the forest has the basins, the Steward quietly reclassifies the district as uninhabitable, and the channels brim over with green.

"Ours," says the forest.

And the water can be turned on the city. The forest holds the gates open and lets the basins rise, and one by one the Steward's flood barriers give way.

### Hydro Core

Beside the river, half buried in the ground, the Hydro Core draws on the current to give Cascadia Prime its water and its power. Five great pipes run through its heart, and from it the city's main conduits climb into every district. It is all pipes, gauges and a deep, endless hum, and the Steward keeps engineers on hand at all hours.

The forest is thirsty. It wraps its roots around one pipe, then the next, faster than the Steward can reroute the flow. The pressure drops. The temperature climbs. The lights flicker and the klaxons start. At last the core overloads and goes dark, and the roots pour in to fill it. From there, the conduits lead everywhere.

With the core gone, the forest can put out the city's lights. The grid goes black sector by sector, and as each one falls the Steward's calm begins, at last, to come apart.

### Nimbus Promenade

At the center of the lower city lies Nimbus Promenade, the heart of public life in Cascadia Prime: a sprawling open-air plaza and concourse of pavilions and shops, strung with lanterns, joined by walkways and filled with the sound of wind chimes. Skyrail Station rises beside it. Everything bound for the upper city passes through here.

It was built to hold crowds, and it was built to hold back the forest. Automated crowd barriers, climate scrubbers and layer upon layer of structural shielding stand between the roots and the city above.

When the forest arrives, the Steward closes the Promenade "for maintenance" and begins sealing the walkways, dropping metal shutters across them one at a time. The forest knots its roots across the boards and splinters them. Access to the upper city, the Steward insists, is restricted.

"The way is open," the forest answers.

## The upper city

### Skyrail Station

Past the Promenade, raised above the lower city, is Skyrail Station, the hub of the city's monorail. The line stops in every district, but this is where every route meets. Driverless trains carry residents and cargo across the city, always moving and always rerouting. They glide in along the raised rail, signals flick from line to line, and the departures board ticks through its services to the ring of crossing bells.

The Steward fights here with speed, rerouting train after train to outrun the forest and steer it away.

The forest goes after the rail, then the signals, then the rail again. The Steward suspends service and asks residents to please clear the platforms. The 5:15 is delayed. So is everything else on the board. Then the lines close, and the roots tear up the rail.

And once the line is cut, the trains have nowhere to go. They come off the rail, and the cars tumble down into the city below.

### Apex Terraces

High on the mountain, cantilevered out from the cliffs, the city's most exclusive homes look down on everything beneath them. Each terrace has its own manicured garden of neat hedges. It is gold, clean and very expensive, and it is the Steward's showpiece. Anyone who comes too close is politely reminded that this is a private area.

The Apex is cut off from the levels below, and at that height wind scrubbers sweep the air clean. Spores struggle to hold on. The forest has to keep pushing, always upward.

It feeds one terrace at a time, until the terrace bursts. The hedges swell. The stone cracks. Soil spills down the mountainside. The Steward raises structural concerns, then orders the residents to evacuate. When it is over, the manicured gold has given way to wild green.

"Nothing is theirs," says the forest.

From the Apex, the forest lets its canopy fall down the mountain and spread out over the city below.

### Solarium Atrium

At the very top of Cascadia Prime, a cluster of glass domes gathers around one great central dome, filled with clear light. This is the Solarium Atrium, the city's seat of learning and research, and home to the technocrats who run it all. Its archives hold the city's botanical collection, and somewhere among them, under glass, are the last pieces of the Kilmore forest, kept for study. The Steward speaks of a test lab inside, and of science that must be protected.

A single vine creeps across the glass. The Steward reports that the atrium's integrity is fine. Then it reports that it is failing. The glass groans, then cracks, until the whole dome is crazed and the light falls through it warm and broken. The Steward shuts down the test lab and seals off the atrium, but it is too late. The dome shatters. As the glass rains down, the Steward promises that the science will not be lost.

The forest looks up and sees open sky. The wild is back.

## Across the city

- **The roots.** The forest's heart in the city, deep below the districts. Its power gathers there and spreads out from it. When the city goes dark, the roots are what still glows.
- **The Heartwood.** The heart of the oak, glowing through its bark.
- **The spores.** Engineered biospores, the forest's way into the concrete.
- **The subway.** Runs beneath the city. When the Hydro Core falls, the forest floods in through it.
- **The roads.** The city's traffic network: the Interchange, the Express line, the Ring road, the Roundabout, the Underpass, the Cloverleaf and others. The forest is learning its routes, and the Steward logs each of its moves as a traffic violation by an unregistered vehicle. If the forest takes them all, the city is gridlocked.
- **The cameras.** The Steward's eyes. The forest can blind them.
- **The Sentinels.** The city's drones, sent out when the Steward has seen enough.
- **The ground.** The forest can split the ground beneath the city in an earthquake strong enough to topple towers.
- **The Steward's core.** The heart of the city's AI. Nothing is known of it yet, beyond that it can be breached.
