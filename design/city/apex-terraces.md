# Apex Terraces

**Status:** revisit. Homes alone don't give it a signature interaction.

## The place

The city's most exclusive homes, cantilevered from the cliffs high on the mountain and looking down on the city. Each terrace has a manicured garden. It's gold, clean and expensive, and it's the Steward's showpiece. It's cut off from the levels below, and wind scrubbers sweep the air clean at that height, so spores struggle to hold on.

## Signature

To decide. What the place has today:

- **The climb.** It's the highest homes, on a mountain.
- **The wind.** Scrubbers strip the spores away, so the forest has to keep pushing.
- **The gardens.** Manicured hedges that swell and burst.

## Gameplay

**Direction:** takes Skyrail Station's old rules, since Skyrail is becoming the roving train.

1. Lift ramp, drops, lift ramp, drops, lift ramp.
2. The third lift ramp shot adds a ball.
3. Then hit 5 drop targets to finish.

The theme to work out. One reading: the lift ramp in its up position is the lift up the cliffs to the terraces, and the drop targets, aimed at from the upper flipper at the top, are the terraces' manicured hedges.

**Before (Apex's old rules):** one shot is lit. Each hit fills its ring. A full ring bursts the terrace, and the lit shot jumps to the far side. Take too long and the ring drains (the wind). Terraces need 3, then 4, then 5 hits. Burst 3. These are parked in [Spire Core](spire-core.md) for now.

## Lighting

**Today:** gold. Bands step up the playfield at the start, the next segment to drain flickers, and a burst is a green wave then a gold surge to the next shot. At completion, gold breaks into green.

## Sound

- Clean, expensive chimes, going out of tune as the terraces fall.
- Creaks and cracking stone with each hit.
- Recorded: `residents-evacuate-now`, `apex-terraces-offline`, `oak/nothing-is-yours`.

## Display

- Start: the terraces on the cliffs.
- Complete: soil spilling down the mountain.
