# Spread roots

**Status:** a single feature today. To be built out into a progression of its own.

The forest's second progression, alongside the climb through the city. Where the city progression is the forest taking places, this is the forest growing: its roots spreading through the city below.

## Today

- Orbit hits grow roots. The orbit hexes' outer rings fill one segment per hit, 6 to a ring. The rings start over each ball.
- A full ring spreads the roots, scoring their value, and arms **Root Lash** on the action button. It's bronze, and pulses lime while a mode is running.
- Pressing it during a mode counts as 2 shots in that mode. With no mode running, it stays armed. It doesn't fire while the ball is in the shooter lane or the mode select menu is open.
- After a lash, the roots stop growing until 50 more playfield switch hits. A spread while a lash is armed only scores.
- Spreading the roots on all three orbits in one ball lights an extra ball, once a game.
