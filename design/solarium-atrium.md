# Solarium Atrium

**Status:** needs the mystery built around it.

## The place

A cluster of glass domes around one great central dome at the peak of the city. It's the city's seat of learning and research, and home to the technocrats who run the city. Its archives hold the botanical collection, and among it, under glass, are the last pieces of the Kilmore forest. The Steward speaks of a test lab inside, and of science that must be protected.

## Signature

The domes, and what's kept under them.

## The mystery

The Solarium should be where the game's secrets come out. The game drops clues along the way, and the Solarium pays them off. The secrets to reveal:

- What the test lab was working on.
- How it relates to the Kilmore specimens.
- Where the biospores came from.
- What the Steward's core is.

One answer that ties them together: the lab made the biospores from the Kilmore specimens, to grow a living material the city could control. The Steward's core is grown from Kilmore wood. Its maintenance is failing because the roots are calling, and part of the Steward is answering. "The science will not be lost" is the Steward protecting what it is.

Clues other places could drop:

- The Steward calls the spores by a lab catalog number.
- A farm or water report mentions Kilmore genetic markers.
- The Steward's calm cracks during the blackout and it says something it shouldn't.
