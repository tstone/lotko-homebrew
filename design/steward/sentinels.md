# Sentinels

**Status:** built. Change planned: move the drones from the drops to the pops.

The city's drones. When Surveillance goes unchecked, the Steward sends them, and they take over the playfield.

## Today

- Sent by Surveillance, with the Steward's "Sentinels deployed." Each dispatch sends one more drone than the last: 1, then 2, then 3.
- It takes over above everything. A running place is suspended and picks up where it left off once the Sentinels are defeated. Nothing new can start while they're out.
- It waits for modes that use the drops or add balls, and for multiballs, to end first.
- Half the GI goes the Steward's red and the other half stays white.
- The drones are the drop targets. Each bank clear downs a drone. Down them all to defeat the Sentinels.
- A drain ends them.
- **Rewards, for now:** every 2 drones downed lights a lock. 5, 10 and 15 drones give points, then an extra ball, then points. These came from when the drones were a side quest, and need revisiting now that they're a punishment.

## Direction

- **Use the pops as the targets instead of the drops.** Pops are more dangerous, which fits drones, and it leaves the drops for places such as Cargo Quay.
