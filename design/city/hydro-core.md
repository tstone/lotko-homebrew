# Hydro Core

**Status:** the best-themed place. Keep it, but it's too hard for tier 1, so move it up the map.

## The place

The city's water supply, half buried beside the river. Five great pipes run through its heart, and the city's main conduits climb from it into every district. The Steward keeps engineers on hand.

The power that used to come from here moves to the [Caldera Generator](caldera-generator.md), so the Hydro Core is only water.

## Signature

Five pipes under pressure, and the Steward rerouting the flow before the forest can drink it dry.

## Gameplay

**Today:** make the combo in order: lift ramp, arc ramp, left orbit, center orbit, dome ramp. Each shot after the first has 25 seconds. Run out and the combo starts over, with 5 more seconds each try.

**Direction:** keep the rule. Moving it later on the map is what fixes the difficulty, but it could also ease up:

- Keep the shots made when the timer runs out, and only lose the current one, as the Steward reroutes one pipe at a time.
- Or let the timer reset the combo only after the second miss.

## Lighting

**Today:** cyan. A coolant chase runs in combo order. The timer is on the arc ramp and speaker rings, and the next shot flashes faster as time runs out. A white-hot surge runs from each made shot to the next, and made shots stay green.

**Direction:**

- The five pipes should read as one system: the made pipes stay green and the next pipe pulses with the pressure.
- Pressure dropping is the timer, so let the whole playfield's cyan dim as time runs out, not just the next shot.
- Completion: the core going dry. Cyan drains away down toward the drain and the roots fill the space it leaves.

Without the power, the lights shouldn't go dark here. That belongs to the Caldera Generator.

## Sound

- A deep hum that rises with each pipe taken.
- Klaxons as the pressure drops.
- Recorded: `coolant-core-dropping`, `core-temperature-rising`, `core-offline`, `oak/drink`. "Coolant" and "core offline" lean toward power, so they may need re-recording around water pressure.

## Display

- Start: the core cutaway with its five pipes.
- Progress: a pressure gauge falling with each pipe.
- Complete: the roots filling the empty core.
