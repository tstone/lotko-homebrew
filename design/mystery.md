# The mystery

**Status:** draft.

Every place in tiers 2 and 3, and the Caldera Generator, drops a clue. Some are small and some are big. A player has to play them all to get every piece, and the finale reveals the whole truth.

## The secrets

- What the Solarium's test lab was working on.
- How it relates to the Kilmore specimens.
- Where the biospores came from.
- What the Steward's core is.

## The answer (draft)

The founders didn't destroy the Kilmore forest completely. They kept pieces of it in the Solarium and studied them. The lab learned to grow Kilmore cells into biomatter, and the city has been putting it into its residents ever since. The biospores came from the same lab, made for the city's own use. The Steward's core is grown from Kilmore wood: a living computer, because nothing else could run a city this complex.

The city is failing now because the roots are calling, and part of the Steward is answering.

## Clues by place

| Place | Size | Clue |
|---|---|---|
| Hydro Core | Small | The Hydro Core has been piping water to a sealed sublevel deep under the city all along. The city has been watering something down there. |
| Helix Vaults | Big | The biomatter is "specimen line K". The residents' enhancements are grown from the forest the city cleared. |
| Skyrail Station | Small | An unscheduled line on the departures board: restricted freight running from the Helix Vaults to the Solarium. |
| Apex Terraces | Small | The terraces' manicured hedges aren't ordinary plants. They're vat-grown Kilmore cultivars: the wealthy decorate with the forest they destroyed. |
| Halcyon Vents | Big | The spores in the city's air aren't only the forest's. The Steward has been releasing biospores through the vents. |
| Caldera Generator | Big | As the power fails, the Steward's voice breaks, and for a moment it speaks like the forest: many voices at once. |
| Solarium Atrium | Reveal | The lab, the specimens and the origin of the biospores. |
| Spire Core | Reveal | The Steward's core is Kilmore wood. |
